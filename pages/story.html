The story of LABBRA

LABBRA is an anonymous acronym that honors six students and pedestrians killed in traffic crashes near UCF.

LABBRA Systems was inspired by the LABBRA Intersection Project, a UCF capstone that explored whether AI and machine learning could detect crashes in real time. The company was formed to reduce road fatalities through proactive, data-driven safety work.

We know where people are likely to get hurt.

The tragedy is that we wait until they do.

LABBRA ends that.
