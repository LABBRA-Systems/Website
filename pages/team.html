Benjamin Mingst

Founder & CEO

Benjamin got the idea for LABBRA in college, after rollerblading more than 1,000 miles across Florida. He proposed and led the UCF capstone team behind the LABBRA Intersection Project, then skated another 500 miles to raise funds and keep momentum going. After graduating, he founded LABBRA Systems with a focus on making a real impact on road safety.
